## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Legal AI Assistant and Prompt Catalog
The system SHALL provide an AI assistant powered by Google Gemini with minimized payload context, integrating a catalog of over 350KB of structured Brazilian legal prompts, petition drafts, and procedural strategies.

#### Scenario: Assisting Petition Drafting
- **WHEN** user selects a legal prompt category (e.g., initial petition, contestation, appeal)
- **THEN** system populates the structured template and contextually assists legal drafting with strict human supervision
